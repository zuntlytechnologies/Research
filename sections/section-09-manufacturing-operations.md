# Section 9 — Manufacturing Operations

> **Input from S8:** finished products are made in GMP plants. **This section's job:** understand plant reality and harvest **digital-manufacturing, calibration/validation, IoT, and facility-support** opportunities — enter as a software/IoT/services vendor, never as a plant owner.

## 9.1 How a pharma plant actually runs
- **Design & utilities:** cleanrooms (ISO classes), **HVAC** (air handling — critical, validated), **water systems** (purified/WFI), compressed gases. Utilities qualification is continuous and audited.
- **Validation & qualification:** DQ/IQ/OQ/PQ for equipment; process validation; **computer system validation (CSV)** for any software touching GxP (this is *your* adoption gate).
- **Production:** planning, batch execution, in-process checks, yield/OEE.
- **Warehouse:** GDP storage, temperature mapping, dispensing.
- **Industry 4.0 reality:** most Indian mid-market plants are **paper/hybrid**; MES penetration is low outside the top tier.

## 9.2 Money & power
Plant head owns output + compliance; QA can stop any batch. Downtime, deviations, and audit findings are the expensive events. Capital to build = C4.

## 9.3 Manual workflows & pain (rich for you)
- **Calibration & preventive-maintenance schedules** in Excel/registers (audit-critical, often a finding source).
- Equipment **logbooks** on paper; **temperature/HVAC monitoring** manual or on standalone dataloggers.
- **Validation document** lifecycle in shared drives; protocol/report version chaos.
- Production planning in Excel; deviation/maintenance reactive.

## 9.4 Existing solutions & gaps
MES (Werum/Körber), historian systems, CMMS (global, expensive). **Gaps:** affordable **calibration/maintenance/validation tracker**, **environmental/utility IoT monitoring** with audit-ready logs, and **paperless logbooks** for mid-market.

## 9.5 Opportunity harvest
- **🟢 SaaS:** calibration + preventive-maintenance + validation lifecycle (audit-ready) — **OPP-13**.
- **🟡 IoT:** environmental/HVAC/temperature monitoring with tamper-proof audit trails.
- **🟡 Services:** validation/CSV-as-a-service (climbs to software).
- **🟢 Hybrid:** paperless logbook + IoT + analytics.

## 9.6 Scored opportunity (condensed)
**OPP-13 — Plant digital-ops suite (calibration, maintenance, validation, e-logbooks).** Model: SaaS (+IoT). Capital C2, TTF T2. Market 7 · Severity 8 (audit findings) · WTP 7 · Reg-comp(inv) 5 · Founder fit 7 · Defensibility 6. **Attr ≈ 66 / Risk ≈ 48 → Medium–High.** Wedge: calibration/maintenance (clear ROI, frequent audit finding), expand to validation + IoT.

## 9.7 Weak opportunities to avoid
Full MES build vs. incumbents (heavy, validation burden). Generic CMMS with no pharma/audit specificity.

## 9.8 Customer-discovery questions
- **Plant/Engineering head:** How are calibration/maintenance/validation tracked? What got cited last audit? Is environmental monitoring manual? Would IoT logs be trusted by your QA/auditors?
- **QA head:** What CSV evidence would you require to allow this software?

## 9.9 Key learnings
- Biggest plant-floor pains = **calibration, maintenance, validation docs, environmental monitoring** — all audit-critical and largely manual.
- **Top:** OPP-13 (digital-ops suite).
- **Next:** S10 Quality systems — the compliance heartbeat and your richest services-to-platform terrain.
