# Section 23 — Export Intelligence & Dossier Lifecycle Management

> **Input from S6/S13/S21:** the highest-fit, most-manual, highest-value workflow. **This section's job:** go deep on the dossier lifecycle and consolidate the **flagship platform thesis** (OPP-01/02) plus distributor/tender data products.

## 23.1 The workflows (where the Excel/email/agents/PDFs/shared-drives live)
1. **Regulatory intelligence** — what does country X require for product Y, and what changed? (newsletters/agents/manual).
2. **Country intelligence** — market size, competition, pricing, reimbursement (scattered).
3. **Product-registration strategy** — which product, which country, when (gut + Excel; should tie to patent expiry OPP-20 + tender demand OPP-03).
4. **Dossier preparation & submission** — CTD/eCTD assembly per country (shared drives, Word/PDF, agents).
5. **Variation management** — every change → filing in each market (missed/late = risk).
6. **Renewal management** — periodic renewals across dozens of markets (Excel reminders; misses = lost market access).
7. **Tender intelligence** — discover/track tenders + competitor prices across many portals (manual).
8. **Competitor intelligence** — who registered/won what, where (no source of truth).

## 23.2 Money & power
Exporters pay agents per market + maintain RA teams; agents guard relationships and data (power). Missed renewal/variation = revenue loss. Whoever **structures this data** owns a durable asset.

## 23.3 Pain ranking
1. Renewal/variation tracking across markets (deadline-critical).
2. Country+product selection blindness.
3. Dossier assembly/version control & reuse across markets.
4. Distributor discovery/vetting.
5. Tender discovery + bid pricing.

## 23.4 Existing solutions & gaps
Veeva Vault RIM (enterprise), local agents, consultants, Excel. **Gaps:** **mid-market dossier-lifecycle + multi-market intelligence** that is affordable, data-rich, and AI-assisted; **distributor-discovery marketplace**; **tender data network**.

## 23.5 Opportunity harvest (consolidated flagship)
- **🟢 Platform (services→data→SaaS):** export registration & dossier-lifecycle intelligence (**OPP-01**) + managed RA (**OPP-02**).
- **🟢 AI:** dossier authoring/gap-check/reuse across markets (OPP-21); regulatory-change intelligence.
- **🟢 Marketplace/data:** distributor discovery (OPP-26); tender network (OPP-03).
- **🟢 Fintech:** export-receivable finance (OPP-12).
