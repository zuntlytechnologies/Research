# Section 6 — Regulatory Affairs

> **Input from S5:** molecule/market choices made; now they must clear the **gate**. **This section's job:** map the regulatory machine and harvest RegTech / intelligence / services-to-platform opportunities — your strongest native terrain.

## 6.1 How it really works (alphabet soup, decoded)
- **Bodies:** **CDSCO/DCGI** (India central), **State FDAs** (licenses, Schedule M GMP), **USFDA**, **EMA** (EU), **MHRA** (UK), **WHO** (prequalification, GMP for procurement markets).
- **Standards:** **ICH** (global harmonization), GMP/GLP/GCP/GDP (manufacturing/lab/clinical/distribution practice).
- **Submissions:** **DMF** (drug master file — API), **CTD/eCTD** (common technical document — the dossier format), **ANDA** (US generic), **NDA** (US new drug), India's import/manufacturing licenses, market-specific registrations.
- **Lifecycle:** initial approval → **variations** (any change needs filing) → **renewals** → responses to deficiencies. Inspections → observations (FDA **483**) → **warning letters** → **import alerts** (market shutdown).

## 6.2 Money & power
RA is a cost center that **controls all revenue timing**. Power: regulators (absolute), RA heads (internal gatekeepers). Consultants/agents earn fat fees per market. Delays = lost first-mover revenue.

## 6.3 Manual workflows & pain (huge for you)
- Dossier assembly across modules in shared drives + Word/PDF; **eCTD** publishing outsourced/expensive; submission/variation/renewal tracking in **Excel**; deadline misses; regulatory **intelligence** (what changed in country X) via newsletters/agents; deficiency-response drafting manual; document version chaos and data-integrity risk.

## 6.4 Existing solutions & gaps
Veeva Vault RIM, ArisGlobal, Lorenz eCTD, ddregpharma services. **Gaps:** **mid-market-affordable** RIM (regulatory information management) + eCTD; **AI dossier authoring/gap-checking**; **regulatory-change intelligence** for Indian exporters across 50+ markets; managed RA-as-a-service that climbs to platform.
