# Section 27 — What Pharma CEOs Want Fixed

> **Input from S25/S26:** problems + power. **This section's job:** role-play the C-suite to extract **biggest frustrations, most expensive problems, repetitive/Excel work, and budget priorities** — your GTM targeting list.

## 27.1 Role-by-role (frustration · most-expensive · most-repetitive · Excel-bound · budget priority)
**CEO** — *Frustration:* compliance risk + slow market access. *Expensive:* audit failures, delayed launches, trapped capital. *Repetitive:* board/regulatory reporting. *Budget:* anything reducing regulatory risk + speeding revenue. → OPP-01/02/08.

**QA Head** — *Frustration:* audit readiness, repeat deviations, data integrity. *Expensive:* 483s/warning letters/recalls. *Repetitive:* deviation/CAPA, doc hunts. *Excel:* training, supplier qual, CAPA logs. *Budget:* eQMS, managed audit-readiness. → OPP-08/10/16.

**Regulatory Head** — *Frustration:* renewal/variation tracking across markets, change intelligence. *Expensive:* missed deadlines = lost market. *Repetitive:* dossier assembly. *Excel:* submission/renewal trackers. *Budget:* RIM + intelligence + AI authoring. → OPP-01/02/21.

**Plant Head** — *Frustration:* downtime, calibration/maintenance/validation audit findings. *Expensive:* batch failures, unplanned downtime. *Excel:* calibration/maintenance logs. *Budget:* digital-ops, IoT monitoring. → OPP-13.

**Export Head** — *Frustration:* country/product selection, distributor discovery, tenders. *Expensive:* failed market entries, missed tenders. *Excel:* registration & tender trackers. *Budget:* export intelligence + distributor/tender data. → OPP-01/03/26.

**CFO** — *Frustration:* trapped working capital, FX, long export receivables. *Expensive:* cost of capital, expiry/returns. *Repetitive:* reconciliation, scenario modeling. *Excel:* everything. *Budget:* fintech/working-capital + CFO analytics. → OPP-04/12/31/32.

**Supply Chain Head** — *Frustration:* cold-chain proof, channel visibility, expiry. *Expensive:* product loss, expiry, excursions. *Excel:* reconciliation, expiry. *Budget:* cold-chain IoT, channel visibility. → OPP-05/15.

**Medical Affairs Head** — *Frustration:* slow MLR/MedInfo, KOL tracking. *Expensive:* compliance missteps. *Budget:* grounded AI MedInfo/MLR. → OPP-33.

**Commercial Head** — *Frustration:* MR/doctor ROI opacity, tender pricing. *Expensive:* unproductive field force, lost tenders. *Excel:* tender/doctor lists. *Budget:* field-force effectiveness + tender intelligence. → OPP-03/24.

**Distribution Head** — *Frustration:* reconciliation, credit, returns/expiry. *Excel/WhatsApp:* orders & reconciliation. *Budget:* channel platform + financing. → OPP-15/04.

**Procurement Head** — *Frustration:* vendor qualification/discovery, supply risk, price. *Excel/PDF:* vendor lists, COAs. *Budget:* sourcing intelligence + qualification registry. → OPP-09/16.

## 27.2 The budget-priority synthesis
**Highest willingness-to-pay** (catastrophic-risk or revenue-linked): regulatory market access (OPP-01/02), audit-readiness/quality (OPP-08/10), PV (OPP-07), tender wins (OPP-03), working capital (OPP-04). **These define your beachhead order.**

## 27.3 Key learnings
- CEOs/CFOs fund **regulatory risk reduction, faster market access, and freed-up capital** above all.
- The Excel-bound, deadline-critical, catastrophic-risk processes (renewals, audit readiness, PV, tenders, working capital) are the **best-funded** entry points.
