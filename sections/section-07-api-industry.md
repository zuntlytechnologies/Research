# Section 7 — API Industry

> **Input from S1:** China dependence (~60–70% of KSMs/intermediates) is India's structural weakness and the reason for PLI/bulk-drug parks. **This section's job:** find **sourcing, qualification, trading, and export** openings — without owning a chemical plant.

## 7.1 How it really works
- **APIs** (active pharmaceutical ingredients) made by **chemical synthesis**, **fermentation**, **peptides**, or **biologics**. Made from **KSMs** (key starting materials) + **intermediates** (mostly imported from China).
- **Economics:** capital-heavy (C4), thin-to-moderate margins, environmental compliance is a major cost (**ETP** effluent treatment, **ZLD** zero liquid discharge). Quality via DMF + GMP inspections.
- **India clusters:** Hyderabad, Gujarat (Ankleshwar/Vapi), Vizag, Maharashtra. Leaders: Divi's, Aurobindo (integrated), Laurus, etc.

## 7.2 Money & power
API makers sell to formulators; power sits with low-cost (China) and with quality/reliability. Procurement teams at formulators bear supply risk and FX risk. MSME API makers are capital- and working-capital-starved.

## 7.3 Manual workflows & pain
Sourcing alternate API/KSM vendors via brokers/trade shows/email; **vendor qualification** (audits, COAs, DMFs) by PDF/email; price discovery opaque; supply-risk monitoring manual; import logistics/FX tracking in Excel; quality-agreement management.

## 7.4 Existing solutions & gaps
PharmaCompass (directory/data), trade portals, brokers. **Gaps:** trustworthy **vendor-qualification registry** + **price/supply intelligence** + **sourcing marketplace** with verified DMFs/GMP status; **import-substitution** discovery (find Indian alternatives to Chinese inputs).

## 7.5 Opportunity harvest
- **🟡 Marketplace + data:** verified API/KSM sourcing with qualification data and price/supply intelligence (OPP-09).
- **🟢 Data/registry:** vendor-qualification & audit registry (shared compliance) — **OPP-16**.
- **🟢 Fintech:** working-capital finance for MSME API makers + import financing (→ S16).
- **🟡 Services:** vendor audits/qualification-as-a-service.

## 7.6 Scored opportunities (condensed)
- **OPP-09 — API/KSM sourcing marketplace + intelligence.** Model: marketplace+data. Capital C2/C3, TTF T2. Market 8 · Severity 8 · Founder fit 7 · Defensibility 7 (data+liquidity). **Attr ≈ 70 / Risk ≈ 55 → Medium–High.** China+1 tailwind; liquidity + trust are the hard parts → start with a data/qualification wedge.
- **OPP-16 — Shared vendor-qualification & audit registry.** Data/SaaS. Capital C2, TTF T2. **Attr ≈ 66 / Risk ≈ 45 → Medium.** Solves duplicate audits across buyers; network effect.

## 7.7 Weak opportunities to avoid
Building an API plant (C4, science/ops, ESG burden) — avoid-as-lead. Pure directory with no verification/transaction (no moat).

## 7.8 Customer-discovery questions
- **Procurement head:** How do you find/qualify alternate API/KSM vendors? How long does qualification take? How do you handle a China supply shock? Price intelligence sources?
- **MSME API maker:** Working-capital pain? How do you find buyers?

## 7.9 Key learnings
- Enter API via **information, qualification, transaction, and finance** layers — not chemistry.
- **Top:** OPP-09 (sourcing marketplace), OPP-16 (qualification registry); strong China+1 tailwind.
- **Next:** S8 Formulations — the finished-product world where most Indian companies (and your buyers) live.
