## 17.2 Quantifying the pain (why it's fundable)
- A USFDA warning letter or import alert can cost a company its **largest market** (tens–hundreds of crores) plus remediation. Compliance spend is therefore **insurance against catastrophic loss** — high WTP, non-discretionary.
- Mid-market firms lack big QA teams → **managed compliance** is attractive.

## 17.3 Opportunity harvest
- **🟢 Managed services→platform:** audit-readiness (OPP-08), continuous compliance ops.
- **🟢 AI:** deviation/CAPA/investigation drafting; batch-review-by-exception; data-integrity anomaly detection.
- **🟢 SaaS:** eQMS (OPP-10), training (OPP-14), supplier registry (OPP-16).
- **🟡 IoT:** environmental/equipment monitoring with audit-ready logs (S9).

## 17.4 Scored opportunity (condensed)
**OPP-08 — Managed audit-readiness + AI compliance ops.** Managed services→AI→SaaS. Capital C2, TTF T2. Market 8 · Severity 9 · WTP 8 · Founder fit 8 · Defensibility 7. **Attractiveness ≈ 73 / Risk ≈ 45 → High; Platform.** Services beachhead, recurring managed contracts, productize the playbook.

## 17.5 Weak opportunities to avoid
Document-management-only tools (no workflow/AI); pure consulting with no recurring/managed/product layer.

## 17.6 Customer-discovery questions
- **QA head/CEO:** What's the all-in annual cost of compliance + remediation? Cost of your last 483/warning letter? Which process eats the most QA hours? Would you outsource audit-readiness as a managed service? What blocks AI in GxP (validation, data integrity)?

## 17.7 Key learnings
- Compliance is a **quantified, catastrophic-risk-backed** spend → ideal for managed-services-to-platform with AI.
- **Top:** OPP-08 (managed audit-readiness), reinforcing OPP-10/14/16.
- **Next:** S18 Pharmacovigilance — mandatory, global, labor-intensive (services→AI platform).
