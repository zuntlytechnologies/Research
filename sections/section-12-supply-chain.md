# Section 12 — Supply Chain

> **Input from S11:** channel power + sell-out visibility. **This section's job:** map medicine movement and harvest **cold-chain IoT, distributor-tech, expiry-reduction, logistics, and pharma-fintech** opportunities — a dense, high-fit cluster.

## 12.1 How medicine moves (and where value is captured)
```
Manufacturer → C&F agent → Stockist → Distributor → Retailer / Hospital / E-pharmacy → Patient
```
- **Margins (indicative, India trade):** retailer ~16–20%, stockist/distributor ~8–10% on many products (DPCO-regulated for scheduled drugs); set by trade norms.
- **Credit cycles:** 30–90 days through the chain → **working capital trapped** everywhere.
- **Cold chain:** vaccines, biologics, insulin need 2–8°C; excursions ruin product and are often detected late.
- **Expiry & returns:** significant value lost to expiry/near-expiry; returns reconciliation is painful.
- **Counterfeit risk:** fuels serialization (→ S20).

## 12.2 Money & power
Channel holds reach + credit float (**power**). Manufacturers carry receivables; distributors/retailers are working-capital-starved; logistics/cold-chain partners bear excursion risk. **Value leaks** at every manual reconciliation and every expired pack.

## 12.3 Manual workflows & pain
- **Cold-chain monitoring** via dumb dataloggers/manual logs; excursions reconstructed after the fact.
- Distributor ordering on **WhatsApp/phone**; manufacturer–C&F–stockist **reconciliation in Excel**; schemes/returns/expiry manual.
- **Expiry management** reactive; near-expiry redistribution ad hoc.
- **Inventory visibility** stops at primary sales; secondary/tertiary invisible.
- Working-capital decisions blind to real channel data.

## 12.4 Existing solutions & gaps
Distributor ERPs (Marg, etc.), AIOCD network, cold-chain logger vendors, 3PL. **Gaps:** **real-time cold-chain IoT + analytics** (OPP-05), **channel visibility + reconciliation** (OPP-15), **expiry-reduction** tools, and **channel/distributor working-capital finance** (OPP-04) using transaction data.

## 12.5 Opportunity harvest
- **🟢 IoT/hardware→data→SaaS:** cold-chain monitoring + excursion analytics + compliance proof (OPP-05).
- **🟢 SaaS/data:** channel visibility + reconciliation (OPP-15); expiry-reduction & redistribution.
- **🟢 Fintech:** distributor/retailer working-capital finance (OPP-04), inventory financing (OPP-25).
- **🟡 Infrastructure:** shared cold-chain network (long-term).

## 12.6 Scored opportunities (condensed)
- **OPP-04 — Distributor/channel working-capital finance.** Fintech (origination via channel data). Capital C3 (phase C2). **Attractiveness ≈ 74 / Risk ≈ 55 → High; Fintech.**
- **OPP-05 — Cold-chain IoT monitoring + analytics.** HW→data→SaaS. Capital C2/C3. Market 8 · Severity 9 (product loss + export proof) · WTP 7 · Founder fit 7 · Defensibility 7 (data+install base). **Attractiveness ≈ 72 / Risk ≈ 52 → High; Hardware-enabled.**
- **OPP-25 — Inventory/expiry-financing + reduction platform.** Fintech+data. **Attractiveness ≈ 64 / Risk ≈ 55 → Medium.**

## 12.7 Weak opportunities to avoid
Owning a 3PL/cold-chain fleet from day one (C4, ops); commoditized datalogger resale with no analytics/recurring layer; pure distributor ERP vs. Marg.

## 12.8 Customer-discovery questions
- **Supply-chain/Warehouse head:** Where is working capital trapped? How is cold chain monitored — would digital proof help with export buyers? Expiry/returns loss %? Reconciliation hours/month?
- **Distributor/stockist:** Credit pain? Would you pay for faster financing against receivables/inventory? How do you order/reconcile today?
- **CFO:** Cash-conversion cycle? Appetite for channel financing?

## 12.9 Key learnings
- Supply chain is a **fintech + IoT + data** goldmine; cold chain and working capital are the sharpest pains.
- **Top 5:** OPP-04 (channel finance), OPP-05 (cold-chain IoT), OPP-15 (channel visibility), OPP-25 (inventory/expiry finance), shared cold-chain infra (long-term).
