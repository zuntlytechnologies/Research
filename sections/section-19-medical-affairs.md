# Section 19 — Medical Affairs & Scientific Communications

> **Input from S11/S18:** between commercial and clinical sits medical affairs — growing budgets, weak tooling. **This section's job:** harvest **medical-information, publication, KOL, and RWE** opportunities (mostly 🟡, need medical hires).

## 19.1 How it really works
- **Medical Affairs** bridges R&D and commercial: **Medical Information** (answering HCP queries), **MSLs** (medical science liaisons engaging KOLs), **publication planning**, **scientific communications**, **medical education**, **real-world evidence (RWE)**.
- Growing as products get more complex and payers demand evidence. Budgets rising, especially for biologics/specialty.

## 19.2 Money & power
Cost center funded by pharma; influences KOLs and evidence narrative. Power: KOLs, medical heads, regulators (promotional compliance). Strict separation from sales (compliance-sensitive).

## 19.3 Manual workflows & pain
Medical-information query handling via email + static FAQ docs; KOL engagement tracked in spreadsheets/CRM; publication planning in Excel/PowerPoint; RWE data scattered; promotional-material medical-legal-regulatory (MLR) review slow and manual.

## 19.4 Existing solutions & gaps
Veeva (MedComms/CRM), Within3, MLR tools. **Gaps:** affordable **medical-information automation (AI Q&A on label/literature)**, **KOL intelligence**, **MLR-review AI**, and **RWE aggregation** for India/emerging markets.

## 19.5 Opportunity harvest
- **🟢 AI:** medical-information chat/Q&A grounded in approved label + literature; MLR pre-review.
- **🟡 Data:** KOL identification & engagement intelligence; RWE aggregation.
- **🟡 Services→platform:** medical writing & publication support.
