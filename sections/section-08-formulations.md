# Section 8 — Formulations

> **Input from S7:** API becomes finished dosage. **This section's job:** understand formulation types and find **packaging, QA/QC-support, CDMO-support, distribution/export** openings — manufacturing-adjacent, not manufacturing.

## 8.1 How it really works (dosage forms + difficulty)
| Form | Process | Difficulty / barrier | Adjacent opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tablets/capsules (oral solids) | Granulation, compression, coating | Low–med; most crowded | Packaging, QA/QC SaaS |
| Injectables/steriles | Aseptic, lyophilization | High (sterility, capital) | Cold chain, serialization, QA |
| Ophthalmics | Sterile | High | QA, packaging |
| Topicals | Mixing | Low–med | Packaging |
| Respiratory (inhalers) | Device + drug | High (complex generic) | Device data, QA |
| Vaccines/biologics | Bio-process | Very high | Cold chain, serialization |
| Controlled-release / complex generics | Specialized | High; **higher margin** | Analytical/QA support |

**Insight:** plain oral solids are commoditized; **complex generics, injectables, and biosimilars** carry margin and regulatory moat — and need more **QA/QC, analytical, cold-chain, and serialization** support (your adjacency).

## 8.2 Money & power
Formulators earn on filings + cost + brand (domestic) or scale (export). Power: regulators, large buyers, channel. Capital is C3–C4 to build a plant.

## 8.3 Manual workflows & pain
Batch manufacturing records (paper/hybrid); QC testing logs; stability study scheduling in Excel; packaging artwork/label version control + regulatory text changes; tech transfer between sites by document handoff; vendor COAs by PDF.

## 8.4 Existing solutions & gaps
MES/LIMS/QMS for the top tier. **Gaps:** affordable **artwork/label management** (huge recall risk source), **stability-study management**, **QC data/LIMS-lite**, and **CDMO-support** tooling for mid-market.

## 8.5 Opportunity harvest
- **🟢 SaaS:** packaging-artwork & label lifecycle management (regulatory-text-aware); stability-study scheduler; QC/LIMS-lite.
- **🟡 Services:** analytical method/QA support; CDMO project management.
- **🟢 Hardware-adjacent:** packaging-line vision/serialization (→ S20).

## 8.6 Scored opportunities (condensed)
- **OPP-22 — Packaging artwork & label lifecycle management.** SaaS. Capital C2, TTF T2. Market 7 · Severity 8 (recall/regulatory risk) · WTP 7 · Founder fit 8 · Defensibility 6. **Attr ≈ 68 / Risk ≈ 45 → Medium–High.** Label errors cause recalls/import alerts — real pain.
- **OPP-23 — Stability-study management SaaS.** Capital C1/C2, TTF T2. **Attr ≈ 60 / Risk ≈ 40 → Medium.** Clear Excel-to-SaaS wedge.

## 8.7 Weak opportunities to avoid
Building a formulation plant (C3–C4, ops/quality risk) — avoid-as-lead. Generic MES vs. incumbents.

## 8.8 Customer-discovery questions
- **QA/Plant head:** How do you manage label/artwork versions and regulatory text changes? Ever had a labeling recall? How are stability schedules tracked?

## 8.9 Key learnings
- Play formulation via **packaging/label, stability, QC, and CDMO-support** software/services.
- **Top:** OPP-22 (artwork/label), OPP-23 (stability).
