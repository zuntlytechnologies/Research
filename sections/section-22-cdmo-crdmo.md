# Section 22 — CDMO & CRDMO Deep Dive

> **Input from S1/S7/S14:** "China+1" + outsourcing tailwind makes India a rising CDMO/CRDMO hub. **This section's job:** harvest **enablement, marketplace, and tech-transfer-tooling** opportunities **without owning a plant.**

## 22.1 How it really works
- **CDMO** = Contract Development & Manufacturing Org; **CRDMO** adds Research (discovery→dev→mfg). Outsourcing types: **discovery, development, manufacturing, analytical, packaging.**
- **Economics:** asset-heavy (C4–C5 plants), but margins good when capacity is utilized; "stickiness" via regulatory filings tied to the site. Growth: patent cliffs, China+1, innovators outsourcing. India leaders: Syngene, Divi's, Aurobindo, Piramal Pharma, Cohance, etc.
- **Customer acquisition:** relationship + reputation + regulatory track record + BD teams; long sales cycles; RFQ/quote-heavy.

## 22.2 Money & power
Sponsors hold demand; CDMOs compete on capacity, quality, IP-security, speed. Power: regulators, large sponsors. Idle capacity = lost money; finding the right partner is slow and opaque.

## 22.3 Manual workflows & pain (your openings)
- **Capacity discovery & RFQ** between sponsors and CDMOs is offline, broker-driven, opaque.
- **Tech transfer** between sites = document chaos, repeated errors, long timelines.
- **BD/quote management** in Excel/email; project tracking manual.
- Capacity utilization not visible/marketed.

## 22.4 Existing solutions & gaps
Brokers, consultants, directories (PharmaSources/PharmaCompass). **Gaps:** trusted **capacity-discovery + RFQ marketplace** (with verified GMP/regulatory status), **tech-transfer management** tooling, and **CDMO BD/quote** SaaS.
