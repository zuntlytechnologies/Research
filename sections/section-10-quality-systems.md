# Section 10 — Quality Systems

> **Input from S9:** quality can stop any batch and is the existential function. **This section's job:** map QMS processes and harvest the **eQMS, managed-compliance, and audit-readiness** opportunities — arguably your single best founder-fit cluster.

## 10.1 How it really works
- **QA** (assurance — systems/release) vs. **QC** (control — testing) vs. **Microbiology**. Core processes: **deviation** management, **CAPA** (corrective/preventive action), **change control**, **OOS/OOT** (out-of-spec/trend) investigations, **stability**, **analytical method validation**, **supplier qualification**, **training/competency**, **document control**, **data integrity** (ALCOA+), **audit/inspection readiness**.
- **Why it exists:** regulators require a documented, traceable quality system; failures → 483s → warning letters → import alerts → market loss.

## 10.2 Money & power
QA head holds veto over release and over *your software*. Quality spend is **non-discretionary**. Cost of failure (recall, import alert) can be tens of crores+ and reputational.

## 10.3 Manual workflows & pain (the motherlode)
- Deviation/CAPA/change-control on paper or hybrid; investigations slow, repeat findings; **CAPA effectiveness** rarely tracked.
- Training records in registers/Excel; competency gaps at audit.
- Supplier qualification duplicated across companies (→ OPP-16).
- **Audit/inspection readiness** = frantic manual document hunts; data-integrity gaps in spreadsheets.

## 10.4 Existing solutions & gaps
MasterControl, Veeva QualityDocs/QMS, TrackWise, Qualio (mid-market). **Gaps:** **India-affordable eQMS** with deviation/CAPA/change-control/training; **AI** for investigation drafting & CAPA-effectiveness; **managed audit-readiness** for mid-market; **data-integrity monitoring**.

## 10.5 Opportunity harvest
- **🟢 SaaS:** mid-market eQMS (OPP-10) — deviation/CAPA/change/training/doc-control.
- **🟢 AI:** investigation & CAPA drafting, root-cause suggestions, data-integrity anomaly detection.
- **🟢 Managed services→platform:** audit-readiness & data-integrity as a service (OPP-08).
- **🟢 Data/registry:** shared supplier-qualification (OPP-16).
- **🟢 Training:** GxP/data-integrity workforce training (OPP-14).

## 10.6 Scored opportunities (condensed)
- **OPP-10 — Mid-market eQMS SaaS.** Capital C2, TTF T2. Market 8 · Severity 9 · WTP 8 · Reg-comp(inv) 4 · AI 7 · SaaS 9 · Founder fit 8 · Defensibility 7 (switching). **Attractiveness ≈ 74 / Risk ≈ 48 → High; Platform.** Adoption gate: CSV/validation, QA trust → sell validated, India-priced.
- **OPP-08 — Managed audit-readiness + data-integrity.** Managed services→SaaS. Capital C2, TTF T2. **Attractiveness ≈ 72 / Risk ≈ 45 → High; Platform.** Services beachhead → continuous managed compliance → platform.

## 10.7 Weak opportunities to avoid
Head-on enterprise eQMS vs. MasterControl/Veeva at top tier; document-only tools with no workflow/AI.

## 10.8 Customer-discovery questions
- **QA head:** Where are deviation/CAPA/change-control today (paper/Excel/tool)? Repeat findings? How do you prepare for a USFDA/WHO audit? Data-integrity worries? What CSV evidence would you need to adopt new software? Who else must approve?
- **QC head:** OOS/OOT investigation pain? LIMS or spreadsheets?
