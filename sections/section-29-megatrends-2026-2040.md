## 29.2 Where to aim a lifetime company
The durable intersection of trends + your fit:
> **An AI-native, data-networked pharma-enablement platform** that starts in **export/regulatory/quality compliance**, accumulates **proprietary regulatory/export/channel/vendor data**, and compounds into **pharma fintech and supply-chain infrastructure** — riding *AI-native pharma + India-as-export/CDMO-hub + supply-chain digitization + track-and-trace + pharma fintech* simultaneously.

## 29.3 Losers to avoid being
Manual-only service shops; analog distributors; commodity API/generic without cost edge; non-compliant exporters; "AI wrappers" without data moats.

- The biggest tailwinds for **you**: AI-native back-office, India export/CDMO ascent, supply-chain digitization, serialization, pharma fintech.
