# Section 5 — Intellectual Property

> **Input from S1:** patent cliffs transfer billions from innovators to generics; India's whole generic engine times itself to expiries. **This section's job:** turn IP into a **data-intelligence** opportunity — a perfect non-scientist founder fit.

## 5.1 How it really works
- **Product patent** (the molecule) vs. **process patent** (how it's made). Innovators stack patents (molecule, polymorph, formulation, use) to extend exclusivity ("**evergreening**" — limited in India by **Section 3(d)**).
- **Patent cliff:** when key patents expire, generics enter and price drops 80–95%. Knowing *exactly* when/where a molecule goes off-patent (per country) = money.
- **Levers:** Paragraph IV challenges (US), **compulsory licensing** (India used it once, Nexavar/Natco 2012), litigation, licensing & royalty deals, technology transfer.

## 5.2 Money & power
Innovators earn monopoly rents; generics earn by being **first-to-file/first-to-launch** at expiry; IP lawyers and patent analysts earn fees. Power = whoever owns/cracks the patent + freedom-to-operate (FTO) analysis.

## 5.3 Manual workflows & pain
Patent landscaping and expiry tracking via paid databases + manual analyst work; FTO opinions in PDFs; per-country expiry mapping in Excel; portfolio prioritization by gut + spreadsheets.

## 5.4 Existing solutions & gaps
Derwent, PatSnap, Orange Book (US), patent law firms. **Gaps:** affordable, **molecule-to-market** intelligence tying patent expiry → regulatory exclusivity → market size → competitor filings, tailored to **Indian generic/export portfolio planning** (which molecule to develop for which country, when).

## 5.5 Opportunity harvest
- **🟢 Data intelligence (your sweet spot):** "patent-cliff-to-launch" portfolio intelligence (expiry + exclusivity + ANDA/DMF filings + market size + competition) for generic/export planners.
- **🟢 AI:** patent/FTO summarization, claim analysis, landscape automation.
- **🟡 Services→platform:** IP analytics & licensing support, then productize.

## 5.7 Weak opportunities to avoid
