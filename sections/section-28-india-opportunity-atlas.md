## 28.2 The "build over a lifetime" reading
- **Start (fast, asset-light, permissionless):** export/tender/IP intelligence, eQMS, managed audit-readiness, PV, calibration/digital-ops, training. (C1–C2, T1–T2)
- **Layer (data network):** convert each into a proprietary data asset (export, tender, channel, vendor, IP). (T3)
- **Compound (fintech + infra):** finance the channel/exporters/suppliers on your data; add cold-chain/serialization infrastructure. (T3–T4, C3+)
- **Endgame:** a **multi-product pharma-enablement platform** (compliance + intelligence + data + finance) — durable, defensible, lifetime-scale.

## 28.3 Key learnings
- The most **founder-fit + defensible + lifetime-scale** categories: **data intelligence/networks, RegTech, quality SaaS, export intelligence, PV, and pharma fintech.**
- **Next:** S29 Megatrends — aim the lifetime company at where the puck is going.
