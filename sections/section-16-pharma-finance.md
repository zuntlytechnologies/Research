## 16.2 Where money is trapped (the fintech map)
- **Distributor/retailer credit float** (30–90 days) → channel financing (OPP-04).
- **Export receivables** (long, FX) → trade finance (OPP-12).
- **Inventory & expiry** → inventory financing (OPP-25).
- **MSME API supplier** working capital + import → supplier/import financing (OPP-30).
- **Compliance-linked risk** → credit scoring using quality/regulatory data as a signal (OPP-31).

## 16.3 Power & reality
Banks/NBFCs hold capital but lack **pharma-specific underwriting data**; that data lives in channel/ERP/marketplace/quality systems — which **you can originate** via your other products. **This is the strategic flywheel:** software/data products → proprietary transaction & risk data → financing on top (highest-margin, most defensible layer).

## 16.4 Manual workflows & pain
Credit decisions on gut + bank statements; reconciliation manual; CFO scenario modeling in Excel; no real-time channel/compliance risk signals.

## 16.5 Opportunity harvest
- **🟢 Fintech:** channel finance (OPP-04), export-receivable finance (OPP-12), inventory/expiry finance (OPP-25), supplier/import finance (OPP-30), compliance-linked credit scoring (OPP-31).
- **🟢 SaaS:** pharma CFO/working-capital analytics & cash-conversion tooling (OPP-32).

## 16.6 Scored opportunities (condensed)
- **OPP-04 — Channel working-capital finance.** **Attractiveness ≈ 74 / Risk ≈ 55 → High; Fintech.**
- **OPP-31 — Compliance-/data-linked pharma credit scoring (B2B2NBFC).** Data+fintech. Capital C2 (data) → C3. Market 7 · Founder fit 9 · Defensibility 8. **Attractiveness ≈ 70 / Risk ≈ 50 → Medium–High; Platform.** Asset-light wedge to fintech.
- **OPP-32 — Pharma CFO / working-capital analytics SaaS.** Capital C1/C2, TTF T2. **Attractiveness ≈ 62 / Risk ≈ 40 → Medium.**

## 16.7 Weak opportunities to avoid
Becoming a sub-scale lender with bank-statement-only underwriting (you'd be a worse bank); generic invoice-discounting with no pharma data edge.

## 16.8 Customer-discovery questions
- **CFO:** Cash-conversion cycle? Where is capital trapped? What financing do you/your channel lack? Would real-time channel/compliance risk data change your credit terms?
- **NBFC partner:** What data would let you lend more/cheaper to pharma SMEs?

## 16.9 Key learnings
- Finance is your **edge**: combine **data origination (from software) + financing** for the most defensible flywheel in this whole atlas.
- **Top 5:** OPP-04, OPP-31, OPP-12, OPP-25, OPP-32.
