# Section 26 — Industry Power Map

> **Input from S2/S25:** stakeholders + problems. **This section's job:** map who controls each lever and identify **entry points that don't require the powerful party's permission.**

## 26.1 Who controls what
| Lever | Controller | Can they block you? | Your entry strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Science** | Scientists, innovators, academia | No (you avoid leading) | Sell tooling/data to them |
| **Approvals** | Regulators (USFDA/EMA/CDSCO) | Indirectly (CSV/validation of your SW) | Build validated, compliance-first products |
| **Manufacturing** | Plant owners, QA | Yes (QA vetoes SW) | Win QA early; mid-market wedge |
| **Quality** | QA heads | Yes | Compliance-first; demonstrate audit value |
| **Exports** | RA heads, foreign agents/regulators | Agents guard data | Services beachhead → own the data |
| **Pricing** | NPPA/DPCO, payers, big buyers | No (you don't price drugs) | Tender/price intelligence |
| **Procurement** | Hospital/govt buyers, procurement heads | They're buyers | Sell them intelligence/tools |
| **Distribution** | Stockists/distributors | Yes (channel power) | Serve the channel (tech+fintech), don't fight it |
| **Patient access** | Doctors/KOLs, pharmacies | No (indirect) | KOL/RWE data |
| **Data** | IQVIA/AIOCD (commercial); fragmented elsewhere | Incumbent in commercial | Build new networks (export/tender/channel/vendor) |
| **Working capital** | Banks/NBFCs | Capital gatekeepers | Partner for capital; you bring data/origination |

## 26.2 Permissionless vs. permissioned entry
- **Permissionless (start here):** data intelligence (export/tender/patent), export renewal tracking, fintech origination SaaS, training, sourcing intelligence — you can build and sell without a powerful gatekeeper's blessing.
- **Permissioned (need to win a gatekeeper):** anything touching GxP release (QA veto), enterprise RIM/QMS (IT/QA), channel financing (needs capital partner), serialization line integration (plant).
- **Strategy:** **start permissionless to build cash, data, and relationships → then enter permissioned cores** with proof and partners.

## 26.3 Power-aware GTM
- Win **QA/RA heads** with compliance-first design + validation packages.
- Win **CFOs** with quantified ROI (cost of audit failure, trapped capital, missed renewals).
- **Serve weak-power nodes** (distributors, MSME suppliers, mid-market exporters) — they welcome enablement.

## 26.4 Key learnings
- Enter at **permissionless, data/finance/services layers**; migrate into permissioned cores once you have leverage.
- QA and channel are the two most common **blockers** — design around them early.
