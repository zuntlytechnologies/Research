# Section 13 — Export Business

> **Input from S6/S12:** regulatory gate + logistics. **This section's job:** map how Indian pharma exports really work and harvest **registration/country intelligence, distributor discovery, tender, documentation, and export-financing** opportunities. (Deep workflow detail continues in S23.)

## 13.1 How it really works
- **~US$27–28B exports (FY24, directional; Pharmexcil/DGCIS).** Market tiers:
  - **Regulated** (US, EU, UK, Canada, Japan, Australia): high barrier (USFDA/EMA), high value, ANDA/dossier-driven.
  - **Semi-regulated** (LatAm, SE Asia, parts of MENA): moderate barrier, registration + agent-driven.
  - **Tender/least-regulated** (Africa, some Asia): WHO-PQ + tenders, price-driven.
- **Process:** pick country+product → register (dossier/CTD per country) → appoint local **distributor/agent** → supply → manage **variations/renewals** → bid tenders.
- **Compliance:** **DGFT** (export policy), **Pharmexcil** (promotion council/RCMC), customs, country-specific.

## 13.2 Money & power
Exporters earn on market access + price; local agents/distributors hold in-country power and guard relationships; foreign regulators gate; tender buyers set price. Receivables are long, FX-exposed.

## 13.3 Manual workflows & pain (your sweet spot)
- **Country/product selection** by gut + scattered data; no integrated view of demand, competition, registration cost, patent expiry.
- **Registration & dossier lifecycle** in Excel + email + per-country agents + shared drives (OPP-01); renewals/variations missed.
- **Distributor discovery & vetting** via trade shows, referrals, embassies — opaque, slow.
- **Tender discovery** across dozens of country portals manual (OPP-03/26).
- **Export documentation** (invoices, COA, CoPP, legalization) repetitive, error-prone.
- **Export receivables** financing limited.

## 13.4 Existing solutions & gaps
Pharmexcil data, consultants/agents, freight forwarders, Veeva (top tier). **Gaps:** integrated **export intelligence** (country + product + competition + registration), **distributor-discovery marketplace**, **dossier-lifecycle platform**, **export-doc automation**, **export-receivable finance**.
