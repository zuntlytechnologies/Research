## 24.3 Cross-cutting AI realities (the adoption gates)
- **Validation/CSV + data integrity:** in GxP, AI must be auditable, explainable, version-controlled, human-in-the-loop. **Start assistive, not autonomous.**
- **Grounding:** regulated content must be grounded in approved sources (label, guidance) — no hallucination tolerance.
- **Defensibility = proprietary data + embedded workflow**, not the model. Own the data exhaust.
- **Buyer trust:** sell outcomes against a known cost (per case, per dossier, per audit).

## 24.4 Weak AI bets to avoid
Non-scientist-led AI drug discovery; autonomous GxP decisioning (regulatory non-starter today); ungrounded medical content; "AI wrapper" with no proprietary data/workflow moat.

## 24.5 Key learnings
- **Best AI = high-volume, mandatory, document/data-heavy back-office** (PV, regulatory, quality, IP, credit) — exactly your strengths.
- **Top 5 AI:** OPP-07, OPP-01/21, OPP-08/10, OPP-20, OPP-31.
