# Section 3 — Drug Discovery & Development

> **Input from S1/S2:** India plays weakly in discovery; your action is in *layers*, not the bench. **This section's job:** understand the top of the funnel well enough to find data/tooling/services openings — and to know where to **avoid leading**.

## 3.1 How it really works (the funnel + brutal economics)
Target identification → hit discovery (HTS / computational) → lead generation → lead optimization (med-chem) → preclinical (tox, ADME, animal) → IND → clinical (S4). **Reality:** ~**10–15 years** and **US$1–2.5B** fully-loaded per approved drug (DiMasi-style estimates, directional); success probability from preclinical to approval is **low single-digit %**. Value is created by *de-risking* a molecule one stage at a time, then licensing/selling.

## 3.3 Money & power
Funded by VC/Big-Pharma/grants; power held by scientific founders, KOL advisors, and capital. Indian biotech is small but rising (NCE/biologics startups, incubators like C-CAMP/BIRAC-funded).

## 3.4 AI in discovery (reality check)
AI drug discovery (e.g., target/molecule generation, protein structure) is real but **science-led and capital-heavy**; outcomes still gated by wet-lab validation and clinical truth. **Founder takeaway:** don't found an AI-discovery biotech as a non-scientist; instead sell **tooling/data/compute/CRO-enablement** to those who do.

## 3.5 Manual workflows & pain
ELN/data scattered; CRO data exchange via email/Excel; literature/IP/target intelligence manual; compound/sample tracking; experiment reproducibility.

## 3.6 Existing solutions & gaps
Benchling, Dotmatics, Schrödinger, CDD Vault (global, expensive). Gap: affordable **India/CRO-facing** data exchange, sample tracking, and literature/target intelligence for the booming Indian discovery-CRO base.

## 3.7 Opportunity harvest
- **🟡 SaaS/Data:** CRO–sponsor data exchange portal; sample/compound tracking; literature & target intelligence (AI).
- **🟡 Services:** discovery-CRO enablement, data management.
- **🔴 Avoid-as-lead:** owning discovery programs.
