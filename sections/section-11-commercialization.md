# Section 11 — Commercialization

> **Input from S2/S5:** demand is controlled by doctors/channel/buyers. **This section's job:** map how products become leaders and harvest **field-force, doctor/channel intelligence, tender, and market-access** opportunities.

## 11.1 How it really works (India specifics)
- **Branded generics** dominate India: same molecule, sold under brands, **prescribed by doctors** influenced by **medical representatives (MRs)** and **KOLs**. Brand + distribution + MR relationships win, not patents.
- **Channels:** retail (prescription-driven), hospital, **institutional/government tenders**, exports.
- **Pricing control:** **NPPA** enforces **DPCO** (Drug Price Control Order) ceiling prices on scheduled drugs; trade margins are regulated for these.
- **Market access:** getting on hospital formularies, government schemes, insurer lists.

## 11.2 Money & power
Margins set by brand strength + DPCO + channel. Power: doctors/KOLs (demand), distributors (reach), tender buyers (volume), NPPA (price). MRs are a huge, hard-to-measure cost.

## 11.3 Manual workflows & pain
- **MR field reporting** via basic SFA or WhatsApp; doctor-call effectiveness opaque; sample/gift tracking; tour-plan compliance.
- **Doctor/KOL master data** messy and duplicated; prescription (Rx) influence hard to measure.
- **Tender discovery** by manually checking many govt portals; bid pricing by gut + Excel; competitor tender history not tracked.
- Channel **sell-out** (secondary sales) invisible; schemes/claims reconciliation manual.

## 11.4 Existing solutions & gaps
SFA tools (many local), IQVIA/AIOCD-AWACS secondary-sales data (expensive), CRM. **Gaps:** affordable **field-force effectiveness + doctor intelligence** for mid-market; **tender intelligence** (OPP-03); **channel sell-out visibility** (OPP-15); **market-access** workflow.

## 11.5 Opportunity harvest
- **🟢 Data intelligence:** tender & competitor-bid intelligence (OPP-03); doctor/KOL & Rx intelligence.
- **🟢 SaaS:** field-force effectiveness (beyond GPS check-in → ROI per doctor); channel sell-out & scheme reconciliation (OPP-15).
- **🟡 Services:** market-access/tender-bid support → platform.

## 11.6 Scored opportunities (condensed)
- **OPP-03 — Tender & govt-procurement intelligence.** Data+SaaS+services. Capital C2, TTF T2. **Attractiveness ≈ 75 / Risk ≈ 42 → High; Platform.** Aggregates fragmented tender portals + competitor pricing history; clear WTP (winning tenders).
- **OPP-24 — Field-force effectiveness + doctor intelligence.** SaaS+AI. Capital C2, TTF T2. Market 8 · Severity 7 · Founder fit 7 · Defensibility 6. **Attractiveness ≈ 66 / Risk ≈ 50 → Medium.** Crowded SFA market → win on *effectiveness analytics*, not check-ins.
- **OPP-15 — Channel sell-out & reconciliation visibility.** SaaS+data. **Attractiveness ≈ 64 / Risk ≈ 50 → Medium.** Gateway to channel fintech (OPP-04).

## 11.7 Weak opportunities to avoid
Another GPS-only MR check-in app (commoditized); reselling secondary-sales data (incumbents own it).

## 11.8 Customer-discovery questions
- **Commercial/Sales head:** How do you measure MR/doctor ROI? Tender win-rate and how you price bids? Sell-out visibility? Scheme reconciliation pain?
- **Tender/govt-business head:** How do you discover tenders and competitor prices today?

## 11.9 Key learnings
- Best commercial plays = **tender intelligence** + **channel visibility** (which feeds fintech).
- **Top:** OPP-03 (tender), OPP-24 (field-force effectiveness), OPP-15 (channel).
