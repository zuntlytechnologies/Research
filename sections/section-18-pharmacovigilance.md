# Section 18 — Pharmacovigilance & Drug Safety

> **Input from S4/S17:** safety monitoring is mandatory globally and labor-intensive. **This section's job:** harvest **case-processing, signal-AI, literature-monitoring, and PV-outsourcing** opportunities — a classic services→AI platform.

## 18.1 How it really works
- **PV** = monitoring drug safety post-approval. Core: **individual case safety reports (ICSRs/adverse events)** intake → coding (MedDRA) → assessment → regulatory submission (within strict timelines); **signal detection**; **aggregate reports** (PSUR/PBRER); **risk management plans (RMPs)**; **literature monitoring**.
- **Regulators:** India **PvPI** (IPC), USFDA FAERS, EMA EudraVigilance, ICH E2B. Mandatory for marketing authorization holders.
- **Reality:** huge case volumes, manual data entry, strict timelines, penalties for non-compliance → heavily **outsourced to India** (PV is a major Indian KPO).

## 18.2 Money & power
MAHs must comply; PV service providers (Accenture, Cognizant, IQVIA, niche PV firms) earn per-case/per-FTE. Regulators enforce. Power: regulators + large outsourcers.

## 18.3 Manual workflows & pain
Case intake from emails/calls/portals/PDFs; manual data entry + duplicate detection; literature screening manual; narrative writing; signal detection in legacy tools; reconciliation across partners.

## 18.4 Existing solutions & gaps
Oracle Argus, ArisGlobal LifeSphere (enterprise safety DBs). **Gaps:** **AI case intake/triage/narrative**, affordable **PV-for-mid-market/exporters**, literature-monitoring automation, and **managed PV** that climbs to an AI platform.

## 18.5 Opportunity harvest
- **🟡/🟢 Managed services→AI:** PV case processing with AI intake/triage/narrative (OPP-07).
- **🟢 AI:** signal detection, literature monitoring, duplicate detection.
- **🟢 SaaS:** lightweight safety-DB/intake for mid-market MAHs/exporters.
