# 04 — Founder-Fit Framework

> **Purpose.** Encode *your* specific founder profile into a reusable filter, so every opportunity is judged against who you actually are — and so we know exactly what to **hire** or **partner** for when an opportunity is attractive but science-heavy.

---

## Part A — Founder profile (the asset base)

**Strengths (lean into these):**
- Finance background → can model unit economics, working capital, valuation; *native fit for pharma fintech, CFO tooling, receivables/inventory finance.*
- Compliance background → understands controls, audit, documentation discipline; *native fit for RegTech, QMS, audit-readiness, PV, serialization compliance.*
- Workflow automation experience → can productize manual processes; *native fit for Excel-to-SaaS, managed-services-to-platform.*
- AI experience → can build AI-native products; *native fit for document/dossier AI, signal detection, intelligence layers.*
- SaaS experience → knows GTM, retention, multi-tenant building.
- Healthcare domain exposure → reduces (not eliminates) the cold-start in a regulated vertical.
- Business/finance/strategy orientation → strong at deal-making, partnerships, capital allocation, roll-ups.

**Gaps (must be hired/partnered/phased — never faked):**
- ❌ Pharma manufacturing experience
- ❌ Chemistry / synthetic-route R&D
- ❌ Biologics development
- ❌ Deep scientific / wet-lab background
- ❌ Clinical science leadership

**Implication:** You should *lead* the **commercialization, compliance, data, distribution, finance, and platform layers**, and *acquire* the science via hires, advisors, JV partners, or by buying a small accredited asset (e.g., a lab or a tech-transfer team).

---

## Part B — Founder-fit scoring rubric (1–10)

Score "Founder suitability for a non-scientist tech/business founder" using these sub-factors (average, with veto rules below):

| Sub-factor | 1–3 (poor fit) | 4–6 (workable) | 7–10 (strong fit) |
|------------|----------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Scientific-leadership dependence | Founder must *be* the scientist | Needs senior scientific hire | Little/no science to lead |
| Regulatory-leadership dependence | Founder must own filings personally | Needs RA hire/partner | Compliance-as-software, hireable |
| Capital wall at entry | >₹25cr to even start | ₹5–25cr | <₹5cr phased |
| Relationship/trust barrier | Decades-deep relationships required | Buildable in 1–2 yrs | Open buying / digital GTM |
| Sales motion fit | Opaque, relationship-only | Mixed | Repeatable, demonstrable ROI |
| Leverage of founder strengths | Uses none | Uses some | Uses finance/compliance/AI/SaaS directly |

**Veto rules (cap founder-fit ≤ 4 regardless of average):**
- Requires the founder to personally hold scientific/clinical/manufacturing credibility that *cannot* be hired or partnered.
- Requires >₹25cr with no phased sub-₹5cr entry.
- Core value depends on pure regulatory arbitrage.

---

## Part C — The "hire / partner / phase / avoid" decision

For any attractive-but-science-heavy opportunity, choose the domain-gap strategy:

1. **Hire** — when the expertise is *operational and ongoing* (RA manager, QA head, analytical chemist, PV physician, customs/DGFT specialist). Build it into the cost model.
2. **Partner / JV** — when the expertise is *asset-bound* (a CDMO's plant, a CRO's site network, a NABL lab's accreditation, a manufacturer's licenses). You bring platform/commercialization/capital; they bring science/assets.
3. **Phase** — when you can start in an *adjacent non-science layer* (data, services, compliance, distribution) and migrate toward the science-heavy core only after earning trust and cash flow.
4. **Avoid-as-lead** — when none of the above neutralizes the gap (e.g., novel drug discovery, gene-therapy development). Participate only as a *supplier/investor*, never as scientific lead.

## Part D — Founder-entry archetypes that fit this profile best

Ranked by natural fit:

1. **Compliance/Reg layer founder** — turn regulatory/quality burden into software + managed services (S6, S10, S17, S18, S20, S23).
2. **Data/intelligence founder** — aggregate trapped data into decision tools (S5 IP/expiry, S11 tender/channel, S21 data, S23 export intel).
3. **Fintech/working-capital founder** — finance the channel/exporters/suppliers using transaction data (S12, S16).
4. **Marketplace/enablement founder** — connect fragmented buyers/sellers with a service wedge (S7 API sourcing, S12 procurement, S22 CDMO capacity, S13 distributor discovery).
5. **Hardware-enabled/IoT founder (with HW partner)** — cold chain, serialization, plant monitoring (S9, S12, S20).
6. **Services-to-platform founder** — start as a services firm, climb the ladder (PV, dossier, audit, validation).

## Part E — Capital-deployment philosophy for this founder

- **Asset-light first.** Prefer software/data/services that start <₹5cr and generate revenue in 6–24 months to fund the journey.
- **Earn the right to go heavy.** Use early cash-flow + relationships + data to *then* enter capital-heavy or science-heavy adjacencies (lab, infra, fintech balance sheet, CDMO JV).
- **Buy vs. build the science.** Acquiring a small accredited lab / RA team / licensed unit can be faster and cheaper than building credibility from zero.
- **Phased capital gates.** Tie each capital escalation to proof: revenue, retention, regulatory acceptance, partner commitment.

## Part F — Master customer-validation question bank (reusable)

Use the relevant subset per opportunity. Goal: validate **problem severity, budget, existing solutions, buying process, switching barriers, implementation, regulatory concerns, ROI, operational willingness, hardware/infra readiness, vendor trust, procurement, and who can block.**

**Universal (any role):**
- Walk me through how you handle [process] today, step by step. Who touches it?
- What breaks most often? What's the last time it caused a real problem?
- How many people/hours does it consume per week/month?
- What do you use today — software, Excel, email, a consultant, nothing?
- If a tool fixed this, who would have to approve buying it? Who could veto it?
- Is there a budget line for this, or would one have to be created?
- What would make you *not* adopt even a great solution? (validation, IT, trust)

**CEO / CFO:** What are your three most expensive recurring problems? Where do audits/recalls/working capital hurt P&L? What's your appetite for new vendors?
**QA / QC Head:** What's your biggest data-integrity / audit-readiness fear? Where is CAPA/deviation/stability still manual? What got cited last inspection?
**Regulatory Affairs Head:** How do you track submissions/variations/renewals across markets? Where do dossiers live (eCTD tool? shared drive?)? Where do deadlines slip?
**Export Head:** How do you decide which country/product to register next? How do you find/vet distributors? How do you track tenders? What's in Excel vs. a tool?
**Plant / Manufacturing Head:** Where do you lose OEE? How is calibration/validation/maintenance tracked? What's on paper logs?
**Supply Chain / Warehouse / Distribution Head:** Where does working capital get trapped? How is expiry/returns/cold chain monitored? Reconciliation pain with C&F/stockists?
**Procurement Head:** How do you qualify/audit API & material vendors? How do you discover alternate sources? Price intelligence?
**CDMO / CRO Head:** How do you win customers? Where is capacity idle? Tech-transfer pain? Quote/RFP workflow?
**Stockist / Distributor / Retail Pharmacy:** Credit terms pain? Expiry/returns? Order/reconcile with companies? Financing needs?
**Logistics Partner / C&F Agent:** Temperature excursions? Documentation burden? Reconciliation/visibility gaps?

## Part G — Founder-fit verdict format (per opportunity)

End each opportunity with a one-line verdict:

> **Founder fit: [score]/10 — [Lead directly / Lead with senior hire / Lead via partner-JV / Phase in from adjacent layer / Avoid-as-lead].** Hire/partner for: [roles/assets]. Sub-₹5cr entry: [yes/phased/no].
