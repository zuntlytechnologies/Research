# 01 — Complete Research Roadmap

> **Purpose of this document.** This is the master map for a 10–20 year, founder-level study of the pharmaceutical industry (global, with deep emphasis on India). It defines *what* we study, *in what order*, *why each section matters to a founder*, and *what opportunity output* each section must produce. Every section feeds the next: stakeholder maps from Section 2 inform money-flow analysis in Section 12, which informs fintech opportunities in Section 16, and so on.

---

## How to use this roadmap

- **You are not studying to become a scientist.** You are studying to become an operator/investor/founder who can *locate* defensible companies and *assemble* the science/regulatory talent through hiring, partnership, or acquisition.
- **Each section is a "deep dive + opportunity harvest."** We learn how the segment really works (not the textbook), then extract opportunities into the **Top 100 Portfolio** (`frameworks/06-top-100-portfolio.md`).
- **Sections are executed one at a time.** After each section we stop, review, and only then proceed. This document is the contract for sequencing.
- **Cross-references compound.** Wherever a later section depends on an earlier one, it is noted as `→ feeds` / `← depends on`.

---

## The four lenses applied to every section

Every section is analyzed through these and tagged accordingly:

1. **Reality lens** — how it *actually* works (who does the work, who controls it, who pays, who suffers).
2. **Money & power lens** — revenue pools, profit pools, bargaining power, value capture.
3. **Friction lens** — what is still manual / Excel / email / PDF / WhatsApp / paper / physical.
4. **Opportunity lens** — SaaS, AI, services, managed services, hardware, IoT, infrastructure, marketplace, export-enablement, fintech, hybrid — each scored and added to the portfolio.

## Per-section deliverable template (applied uniformly)

Each section file (`sections/section-NN-*.md`) will contain, in order:

1. **Industry explanation** (reality, not textbook)
2. **Real-world workflow map**
3. **Stakeholder map** (who does / controls / earns / bears risk / has power / pays / suffers)
4. **Money-flow map**
5. **Power map**
6. **Regulatory burden**
7. **Manual workflows** (Excel / email / PDF / shared drive / WhatsApp / paper / physical / field)
8. **Pain points** (ranked)
9. **Existing solutions & vendors** + why inadequate
10. **Opportunity harvest** by type: AI · SaaS · Services · Managed services · Hardware/Device · IoT · Export · Infrastructure · Marketplace · Fintech · Hybrid
11. **Weak opportunities to avoid** (with reasons)
12. **Top opportunities with scorecards** (using `05-scoring-framework.md`)
13. **Customer discovery questions** (by role)
14. **Key learnings** + biggest pains + most manual processes + largest gaps + Top 5 startup opportunities + recommended interviews + next areas to study

## Evidence discipline (applies to all sections)

- Prefer: CDSCO/DCGI, USFDA, EMA, MHRA, WHO, ICH, NPPA, DGFT, Pharmexcil, IBEF, IQVIA, government filings, company annual reports/investor decks, reputable consulting (McKinsey/BCG/EY/Deloitte), and trade data (DGCIS, ITC Trade Map).
- **Never invent precise figures.** Use ranges and explicitly label assumptions as `[ASSUMPTION]`.
- Mark data freshness; pharma sizing shifts yearly. Treat all market figures as directional unless tied to a cited filing.
